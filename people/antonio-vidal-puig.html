Professor Antonio (Toni) Vidal-Puig

Profile

Investigates how obesity leads to insulin resistance, diabetes and cardiometabolic disease, with a focus on adipose tissue expandability, lipotoxicity and brown fat thermogenesis.

Background and experience

Antonio (Toni) Vidal-Puig is Professor of Molecular Nutrition and Metabolism at the University of Cambridge, a position he has held since October 2009. He is Associate Director of the MRC Metabolic Disease Unit, an MRC Investigator, Lead of Cardiometabolic Medicine at the Cambridge Heart and Lung Research Institute, Honorary Consultant in Metabolic Medicine at Addenbrooke’s Hospital, and Scientific Director of the Cambridge Phenomics Centre.

He obtained his medical degree at the University of Valencia in 1986 and his PhD at the University of Granada in 1992. After clinical training in Granada and research appointments at Harvard Medical School, he joined Cambridge as a Senior Research Associate in 1999. He became Lecturer in 2003 and Reader in 2006 before his appointment as Professor.

This biography is based on his June 2025 CV. Appointments and bibliometric figures reflect that document.

Research focus

His research elucidates the molecular mechanisms linking obesity with insulin resistance, diabetes and cardiometabolic complications, and develops related therapeutic strategies. The laboratory combines hypothesis-driven research with unbiased systems approaches, using animal models, stem cell biology, human biological samples (including induced pluripotent stem cells), advanced omics technologies and bioinformatics integration.

His contributions include the adipose tissue expandability hypothesis and the concept of lipotoxicity; energy-dissipating strategies involving mitochondrial biogenesis, mitochondrial uncoupling and brown fat differentiation and activation; hypothalamic lipid metabolism and the roles of thyroid hormone and BMP8b in energy homeostasis and brown fat activation; and systems biology and stem cell approaches to lipid networks in cardiometabolic disease.

International appointments

Principal Investigator, Principe Felipe Research Center (CIPF), Valencia; Associate Research Scientist, Cambridge University Nanjing Centre of Technology and Innovation (CUNJC); Honorary Professor, University of Nanjing; Visiting Professor, Chiba University; and Honorary Professor, Soochow University (appointments listed in the June 2025 CV).

Additional appointments and affiliations include International Research Affiliate at the Centre on AI Technology for Humankind, National University of Singapore (2019); Toh Chin Chye Visiting Professorship, National University of Singapore (2024); Distinguished Visiting Professor, School of Life & Health Sciences, Fuyao University of Science and Technology (2024); and Fudan Scholar, Fudan University (2025).

Professional history

October 2009 onwards — Professor of Molecular Nutrition and Metabolism, University of Cambridge.

2006–2009 — Reader in Metabolism and Biochemistry, University of Cambridge; Honorary Consultant in Metabolic Medicine; Director of the Wellcome Trust Integrative Physiology Mouse Program; Deputy Director of CORD-MRL (Centre for Obesity and Related Disorders); and Scientific Director of the Cambridge Phenomics Centre.

2003–2006 — Lecturer in Clinical Biochemistry and Metabolic Medicine, University of Cambridge; Honorary Consultant in Clinical Biochemistry and Metabolic Medicine; and Director of the Wellcome Trust Integrative Physiology Mouse Program.

1999–2002 — Senior Research Associate, Departments of Clinical Biochemistry and Medicine, University of Cambridge; Honorary Specialist Registrar in Metabolic Medicine, Addenbrooke’s Hospital.

1997–1999 — Instructor, Department of Medicine, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Harvard Medical School, supported by a Boston Obesity Nutrition Career Development Award.

1993–1997 — Research Associate, Department of Medicine, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Harvard Medical School, with support from the American Heart Association Paul Dudley White Fellowship.

1992–1993 — Postdoctoral Fellow, Department of Medicine, Massachusetts General Hospital, Harvard Medical School, supported by the Spanish Ministry of Health.

1988–1991 — Intern and Resident in Endocrinology and Metabolism, Granada University Hospital, Spain.

Scientific service and teaching

He chaired the ERC Physiology and Endocrinology award panel (2018–2023) and represented the University of Cambridge Clinical School on the Research Policy Committee (2023–2025). The CV also lists membership of the BHF Chairs and Programme Grants Committee (2025–2028) and chairmanship of the European Atherosclerosis Society Working Group for Educational Programme Development (2022).

His scientific advisory service includes CIBERDEM, IDIBAPS, INCLIVA, EASO, IDIBGI, the Danish Academy of Diabetes, the University of Copenhagen’s rodent metabolic phenotyping platform, IDIS and Fundación Gadea Ciencia. Editorial work includes PLoS Biology, Biochemical Journal, Diabetes, Trends in Endocrinology and Metabolism, Biochimica et Biophysica Acta and Life Metabolism.

He teaches obesity, diabetes and cardiovascular topics on Cambridge’s Master’s in Bioscience Enterprise. TVPlab organises the Cardiometabolic Phenotyping in Rodents course, combining expertise at the Institute of Metabolic Science and BHF Cardiovascular Centre with a Sanger/EBI workshop.

Research training and mentoring

The June 2025 CV reports training 18 PhD students and 27 postdoctoral fellows supported by competitive national and international funding. His interests include innovative management in research-intensive institutions, talent development, scientific guidance, career advice and networking. He supports mentoring initiatives for women in STEM through the Academy of Medical Sciences, British Heart Foundation and Wellcome Trust DBT India Alliance.
