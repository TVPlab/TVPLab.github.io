People/Dr. Vanessa Pellegrinelli

TVPlab · Cambridge

Dr. Vanessa Pellegrinelli

Postdoctoral Research Associate

Emailvp332@medschl.cam.ac.uk

Profile

Studies how adipose tissue structure and extracellular matrix remodelling shape tissue plasticity and function, including fibro-inflammation and impaired brown fat thermogenesis.

Explore our research →Back to Our people ↗
On this pageResearch focusBackground and experienceKey publicationsProfessional links

Research focus

By combining in vivo and in vitro models mimicking pathophysiological conditions with imaging and systems biology approaches, I am investigating how impaired adipose tissue plasticity may influence tissue functionality.

Background and experience

I am a Research Associate working on different aspects of adipose tissue structural networks including extracellular matrix remodelling. An integrative physiologist with considerable experience in adipose tissue biology and animal phenotyping. Responsible, hardworking and highly organized with a problem-solving attitude. Easily adaptable to multicultural environments, holding a strong commitment to scientific collaboration.
