People/Dr. Hao Jia

TVPlab · Cambridge

Dr. Hao Jia

Postdoctoral Research Associate

Profile

Studies how lipid metabolism in different macrophage populations influences the progression of fatty liver disease, focusing on membrane remodelling and new lipid synthesis in MASLD and MASH.

Background

Hao completed a Biomedical Engineering PhD at Beihang University and joined TVPlab in 2024 with a Royal Society Newton International Fellowship. The project combines human liver samples with mouse models.
