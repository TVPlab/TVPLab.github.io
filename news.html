News & Events

Latest updates from the TVP Lab — publications, awards, collaborations, and events.

March 2026

New Paper Published in Nature Metabolism

Our latest study on adipose tissue remodelling during prolonged metabolic stress has been accepted and published in Nature Metabolism, revealing novel regulators of adipocyte plasticity.

February 2026

Wellcome Trust Senior Investigator Award Renewed

The Wellcome Trust has renewed Professor Vidal-Puig's Senior Investigator Award, supporting the next five-year phase of research into brown adipose tissue thermogenesis and therapeutics.

January 2026

International Symposium on Adipose Biology — Cambridge

The TVP Lab co-hosted a two-day international symposium at the Institute of Metabolic Science, welcoming leading researchers from across Europe and Asia.

November 2025

PhD Student Wins MRC Prize for Best Thesis

Congratulations to Dr. Sophie Clarke, whose thesis on macrophage polarisation in obese adipose tissue was awarded the MRC Prize for Best Doctoral Thesis in Metabolic Science.

September 2025

New Collaboration with Karolinska Institute Announced

The TVP Lab has announced a new research collaboration with the Karolinska Institute in Stockholm to investigate the role of brown fat in human energy homeostasis using PET-CT imaging.

July 2025

Prof. Vidal-Puig Delivers Keynote at EASD 2025

Professor Vidal-Puig delivered the opening keynote lecture at the European Association for the Study of Diabetes annual meeting, speaking on the future of adipose tissue research.
