Research across borders

TVPlab in Nanjing

Adipose tissue function. Liver health. Collaboration across borders.

Nanjing · China

Connecting mechanisms with metabolic health

TVPlab’s Nanjing programme is based at the Cambridge University–Nanjing Centre of Technology and Innovation. It studies obesity-related cardiometabolic complications, including their relevance to Asian populations.

The Centre’s project page names Antonio Vidal-Puig as principal investigator and Sergio Rodriguez-Cuenca as a researcher.

Explore the Nanjing programme

Research focus

Adipose tissue plasticity

Understanding how healthier adipose tissue can limit ectopic lipid accumulation and its metabolic consequences.

Liver lipid networks

Examining how the amount and composition of hepatic lipids relate to inflammation, fibrosis and fatty liver disease.

Biomarkers and therapeutic targets

Identifying disease mechanisms that may help recognise higher-risk individuals and reveal targets for intervention.
