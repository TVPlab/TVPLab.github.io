About TVPlab

Understanding how
the body uses energy.

Meet our laboratory ↗

We are a biomedical research laboratory led by Professor Antonio Vidal-Puig at the Institute of Metabolic Science, University of Cambridge.

Our research explores the molecular mechanisms controlling energy expenditure, fat deposition, and the partition of energy towards oxidation or storage.
