About Us

We are a biomedical research laboratory based at the Institute of Metabolic Science (IMS) at Addenbrooke's Hospital in Cambridge, led by Professor Antonio Vidal-Puig. Our program of research explores the molecular mechanisms involved in controlling energy expenditure, fat deposition, and the mechanisms controlling the partition of energy towards oxidation or storage.
