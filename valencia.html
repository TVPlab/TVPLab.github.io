TVPlab in Valencia

A CIPF–Cambridge collaboration in metabolic research.

Valencia · Spain

Complementary expertise, shared questions

TVPlab in Valencia grew from the partnership between the Príncipe Felipe Research Centre (CIPF) and the University of Cambridge, co-led by Antonio Vidal-Puig and Deborah Burks.

The collaboration brings together work on obesity, diabetes and their complications. Explore the related CIPF groups below to see their distinct research programmes.
